Shakshuka

Description

This shakshuka recipe is a one-pan meal that is fast, easy, and delicious. Be sure to cook the sauce until the veggies are nice, soft, and sweet. Once the eggs go in, you can finish the dish covered on the stove or pop the pan into a hot oven until it cooks to your liking. No matter how you like your yolks cooked, this makes for an impressive breakfast, brunch, lunch, or dinner.
